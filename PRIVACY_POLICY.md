# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** October 26, 2026

## Overview
This application ("Application") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our mobile or desktop applications.

## Data Collection
* **Personal Data:** We do not collect personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, or phone number) unless explicitly provided by you.
* **Local Processing:** Media inputs, text prompts, or files generated using local models stay on your device unless sent to an external API service for processing.
* **Network Requests:** If the Application connects to third-party AI APIs (e.g., model inference endpoints), your prompts and temporary media assets may be transmitted to those providers in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

## Media & Permissions
* **Storage/Gallery Access:** Requested solely to read input media or save generated images and videos to your device.
* **Camera/Microphone (if applicable):** Used only when actively initiated by the user for capturing content within the app.

## Third-Party Services
This app may integrate with third-party libraries or model hosting services. Please review the privacy policies of those individual services for details on their data retention and handling practices.

## Contact
For questions or concerns regarding this policy, please open an issue in the project repository or contact the maintainer directly.
